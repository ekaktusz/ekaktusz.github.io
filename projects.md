## Elephant In The Room

<p class="project-meta">Game Off 2022 · PICO-8 · Lua</p>

This small puzzle game with multiple levels and mechanics was created as a
submission for Github Game Off 2022 in PICO-8 and Lua. This was my first game
jam. We finished in 4th place overall. I did the coding alone in approximately
10 days, and designed the website.

<p class="project-meta">Ludum Dare 52 · PICO-8 · Lua</p>

jam category. This was our second time with a game jam together. I've done all
the coding alone in 72 hours with Lua and PICO-8. We finished in 65th place
overall from 1632 entries.
